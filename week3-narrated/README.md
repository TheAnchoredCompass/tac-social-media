# Week 3 narrated videos (Nov 4 to Nov 8, 2026)

Moving-panel videos with narration, made Nov 3, 2026 to replace the still images and the earlier TikTok and Shorts narration for Wednesday through Sunday.

- Daily videos (`dayNN_*_daily.mp4`) are used for that day's Instagram, Facebook and X posts. Built from that day's Instagram carousel in `week3/`.
- `*_tiktok.mp4` and `*_youtube-shorts.mp4` replace the Week 3 versions in `week3-video/`.
- Format: 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 and AAC, about -16 LUFS. Cream cards on navy, navy cards on cream.
- Narration: ElevenLabs Eleven v4, socials@ workspace (commercial plan), one take each. Voices: My Cloned Voice 2 (owner's clone, remixed lower) and My Woman's Voice (designed).
- Music: the owner-licensed Pixabay tracks recorded in `week1-music/MUSIC_LICENSES.md`, mixed about 21 dB under the voice.

1. Mon Oct 26, 3 PM, TikTok, post 29 (banter)
Here's the thing about teasing. It only lands when the other person already feels liked. Poke fun at the little stuff, how they always order the same thing, never at what they're insecure about. And if the laugh doesn't come? Let it go. No defending the joke.

2. Tue Oct 27, 3:30 PM, YouTube Short, post 35 (deadlines)
Your boss drops an impossible deadline on you. Most people just say yes and quietly resent it. Instead, put the choice back on the table. Ask which project should slide so this one gets done right. Now it's a shared decision, not your burden to carry alone.

5. Thu Oct 29, 3 PM, TikTok, post 31 (chores)
Most chore fights aren't about chores. They're about keeping score. Instead of "you never help," split things by ownership. You take dinner start to finish; I've got dishes and trash. Nobody's managing anybody, and the resentment has nowhere to grow.

6. Fri Oct 30, 3 PM, TikTok, post 32 (conflict)
When an argument heats up, the fastest way to lose is to make it about the person. "You always." "You never." That's when they stop listening. Name the problem instead, and what you need from them. Now you're on the same side of the table.

7. Sat Oct 31, 1 PM, TikTok, post 33 (family)
Family is where boundaries get hardest, because guilt does the talking. But saying yes when you mean no just builds quiet resentment. A boundary isn't you pushing them away. It's you staying honest. "I love you, and I can't make it this weekend." That's enough.

8. Sat Oct 31, 5 PM, TikTok, post 34 (after hours)
Your boss texts at eleven at night. You don't have to panic-reply, and you don't have to lie awake dreading it either. Unless something's actually on fire, answer in the morning: "Looking at this now, I'll have an update by noon." Calm, clear, and on your terms.

# Voice clone reading script (Instant Voice Cloning sample)

Prepared Oct 24, 2026 for the owner's own-voice clone on the socials@ ElevenLabs workspace.
Read at your natural narration pace; about 2 minutes. Bracketed directions are not read aloud.
ElevenLabs guidance: 1 to 2 minutes of clean audio, one speaker, quiet room, no echo, MP3 at 192 kbps.
https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-cloning/instant-voice-cloning

[Warm, conversational]
Hi, I'm Troy, and this is The Anchored Compass. Most of us don't struggle because we don't care. We struggle because the hard conversations show up at the worst possible time. [pause]

[Thoughtful, slower]
Think about the last text you rewrote five times... and still didn't send. [short pause] What were you actually afraid of?

[Calm, steady]
Here's what I've learned. A boundary isn't a wall. It's a door, and you hold the key. You can say, "I love you, and I can't make it this weekend." That's a complete sentence.

[Practical, even]
Your boss texts at eleven at night? You don't have to panic. Answer at eight: "Looking at this now. I'll have an update by noon."

[Softer, gentle]
And if you're tired, that's okay. Take a breath. You don't have to fix everything tonight.

[Firmer, louder]
But don't let guilt make the decision for you. Not at work. Not at home. Not anywhere.

[Excited, upbeat]
And honestly? When it clicks, it feels amazing. That first calm conversation where nobody storms off? That's a win. Celebrate it!

[Somber, slower]
Some conversations are heavy. A parent who's getting older. A friendship that's fading. A marriage that's gone quiet in all the wrong ways. [long pause]

[Warm, reassuring]
You're not alone in that. You don't need perfect words. You need honest ones.

[Curious, light]
So what's one conversation you've been putting off? [pause] Just one.

[Steady, closing]
Start there. Keep it simple, keep it kind, and stay anchored. I'm Troy, and this is The Anchored Compass.
